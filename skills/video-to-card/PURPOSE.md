# Purpose: video-to-card

## Why it exists

Many sources worth reading are videos (X posts, YouTube talks, interviews) whose substance is spoken, not written. Each one needs the same mechanical work: find the media, transcribe it, look at the frames, file it. Done by hand, each run re-derives the route. The useful step comes after that: turn the video's rules into work on one of the author's cards. The skill makes the mechanical part one script, and makes the card block the expected output, not an afterthought.

## Motivating ideas

- Five hand-made video transcripts, over three weeks, each re-derived the same route: X's public embed endpoint or yt-dlp, ffmpeg, whisper.cpp with the small model, contact sheets of frames.
- A video check of a third-party summary of one video found, of 22 points, 9 supported, 9 partly supported, 3 absent and 1 contradicted. Reading a summary of a video is not reading the video.
- The depth expected in the card: each rule grounded in a wiki fact or a read-only live check. In one run, the live check found two gaps in a project's donation pages before any ad spend.
- The author picks the card. The skill offers at most two candidates and asks once.

## Would be retired when

Video platforms give reliable transcripts with frames to agents directly. Or deep-ingest absorbs the video route and the card block, and removing this skill causes no regression.
