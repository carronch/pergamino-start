# Purpose: teach

## Why it exists

The author wants to understand the systems in the setup, not only to have them summarized, and people often understand best by learning to teach. A summary hands over conclusions; this skill builds the understanding step by step, with the reasons and the history, so that the author can explain it to someone else.

## Motivating ideas

- You can outsource memory and computation; you cannot outsource understanding.
- Anchor the technical term, then explain it plainly.
- pstack's `teach` skill (github.com/cursor/plugins, commit `9d76bca`) showed the shape: definition first, smallest answer first, diagrams that build up.

## Would be retired when

The author stops asking for explanations in this form for three months, or another teaching flow replaces it.
