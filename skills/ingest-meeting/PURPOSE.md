# Purpose: ingest-meeting

## Why it exists

Meetings leave nothing in a knowledge brain unless something turns the transcript into
decisions, action items and links to the organizations in it. This skill turns any
transcript or set of notes into one structured meeting note, and puts the author's own
dated action items in the author's reminders.

## Motivating evidence

- Decisions and action items with an owner and a due date are the parts of a meeting that
  people look for later. A transcript alone does not answer "what did we decide?".
- People get escalations, not inboxes: an action item that someone else owns stays in the
  note and on that organization's page, never in the author's reminders.
- The transcript keeps one durable copy. A second copy in the brain drifts, costs search
  quality, and spreads private content.
- Native capture by the meeting tool, or local transcription, keeps meeting content inside
  tools the author already trusts. A third-party meeting bot adds a new place where that
  content lives.

## Would be retired when

The author's meetings no longer produce transcripts or notes, or the meeting tool itself
writes the structured note into the brain, so this step adds nothing.
